# Template: Listicle

**Use for:** shareable, scannable "N signs / N mistakes / N things" topics (e.g. "5 signs your company has a data problem").

**Arc:**
1. **Hook (0:00–0:45):** promise the list and the payoff, and plant the tension ("number 4 is the one nobody admits to"). No greetings.
2. **Credibility + promise (to ~1:30):** one breath on why the presenter can call these, and what the viewer will be able to spot by the end.
3. **Body — the N items:** each item is a mini-beat: name it, one concrete example, why it matters, and a one-line bridge to the next. Order for momentum, strongest or most surprising kept to re-hook mid-video.
4. **Landing:** pull the items into a single takeaway, give one action, tease the next video (often a deeper dive into one item).
5. **Sign-off:** one line.

**Watch for:** items must not blur into each other; each earns its place; avoid the "list of three" AI tell by varying rhythm; keep the through-line so it's a journey, not a checklist read aloud.
