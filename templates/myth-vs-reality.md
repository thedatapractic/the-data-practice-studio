# Template: Myth vs Reality

**Use for:** topics where the audience holds a common wrong belief worth correcting (e.g. "Excel is not a database", "AI will take your data job").

**Arc:**
1. **Hook (0:00–0:45):** state the myth in the audience's own words, then promise to show why it quietly costs them. Open loop: "here's what's actually going on."
2. **Credibility + promise (to ~2:00):** why the presenter has seen this go wrong first-hand; what the viewer will understand by the end.
3. **Body — myth then reality, in beats:** for each sub-point, name the belief, show why it feels true, then the reality with a concrete lived example and the cost of getting it wrong. Each beat bridges into the next myth.
4. **Landing:** the corrected mental model in 3–4 sentences, one action the viewer can take tomorrow, tease the next video.
5. **Sign-off:** one line.

**Watch for:** never mock the viewer for holding the myth; make the reality land through a specific story, not assertion; keep each myth genuinely distinct.
