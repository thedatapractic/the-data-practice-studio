# Template: Identity / Story

**Use for:** trust-building videos built on the presenter's own experience: career journey, day-in-the-life, war stories (pillar 5). About 1 in 5–6 videos.

**Arc:**
1. **Hook (0:00–0:45):** drop into a specific moment or stakes ("a single wrong date could have changed a medical decision"), not a summary. Promise where the story goes.
2. **Setup (to ~2:00):** who the presenter was at that point and what was on the line, so the viewer has a reason to follow.
3. **Body — the arc:** tell it as a real story with turning points; each beat carries a transferable lesson for the viewer's own work, surfaced lightly, not lectured. Bridges are the "and then" of the story.
4. **Landing:** the lesson the whole story earns, one thing the viewer can apply, tease the next video.
5. **Sign-off:** one line.

**Watch for:** this template leans hardest on real, approved anecdotes, mark `[PLACEHOLDER: ...]` for anything not in the anecdotes bank; keep the lesson honest to what actually happened; the presenter is the authority here, not cited sources.
