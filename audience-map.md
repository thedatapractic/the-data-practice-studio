## How to read it

**Audience.** **S** = students and aspiring data professionals. **P** = practising data professionals. **Both** = genuinely serves each without weakening for either.

The honest answer to your question is **no, not every title suits both audiences, and that is fine.** A video that tries to serve both usually serves neither: the student wants orientation and the professional wants a fix, and those are different videos. What matters is the balance across the library, which is assessed at the bottom.

**Subject area.** DM = data management · DG = data governance · DA = data analytics · SP = SQL and Python. **Careers** is marked separately because it cuts across all four rather than being a subject of its own, and it is the main way the student audience is reached.

## Balance

Counting the lean rather than the ideal, against the new numbering:

- **Students first: 11** — pure-S videos 3, 5, 6, 7, 10, 21, 28 (7), plus 15, 20, 29, 31 leaning that way (4)
- **Professionals first: 13** — pure-P videos 2, 4, 8, 9, 11, 13, 17, 18, 23, 26, 27 (11), plus 14, 25 leaning that way (2)
- **Genuinely both, no lean: 7** videos (1, 12, 16, 19, 22, 24, 30)

**Verdict: still tilting towards professionals, and the two new items this run reinforce that tilt rather than correct it.** Both #8 (Garbage In, Garbage Out) and #26 (AI agents accountability) were scored **P-leaning**, because both genuinely are: upstream data-entry discipline and agentic-pipeline accountability are organisational problems first. That is an honest scoring, not a drift to be hidden, but it means **two P-leaning additions in one run, with zero new S-leaning additions**, is worth Aji's attention. #31 ("Data Management vs Data Governance"), still awaiting her approval, would help correct this if approved — it scores **Both, leans S**, since the confusion it resolves is one a job-hunting student hits before a working professional does. **Recommendation: if Aji wants to actively rebalance rather than just watch it, approve #31 next among the proposed items, ahead of #8 and #26,** since it is the only one of the three pulling towards the underweight side.

The student-facing videos remain clustered in careers content rather than spread across the subjects, which means a student who is not asking a careers question still has fewer entry points than a professional with a felt problem does. This was true in the 2026-08-03 assessment and remains true now; no video added since then has closed it.
